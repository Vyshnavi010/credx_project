# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

This document outlines the target profile, customer triggers, online hubs, and specific launch tactics to get the first 100 users for the AI Spend Audit tool with a $0 budget.

---

## 1. Target User Profile (ICP)
Our primary target user is the **Fractional CTO**, **VP of Engineering**, or the **Lead Engineer (employee #1–5)** at a pre-seed to Series A startup (5 to 30 people) that is building AI-native features.

### Why this specific persona?
- **Fractional CTOs** manage budgets for 3–5 early-stage startups simultaneously. They are judged on capital efficiency. Saving $300/mo per startup directly demonstrates their consulting value.
- **VPs of Engineering** at Series A startups are pressured by founders/VCs to reduce SaaS burn.
- **Seed founders** are cost-conscious, but usually lack the engineering oversight to notice duplicate seats (e.g. Cursor Pro + GitHub Copilot Business on the same developer's computer).

## 4. Path to 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)

We will execute three highly targeted outreach tracks:

### Track 1: Fractional CTO Direct Outreach (Days 1–10)
- Extract a list of 50 Fractional CTOs in SF/NY/London using LinkedIn filter strings.
- Send a personalized, zero-sales message:
  > *"Hey [Name], built a quick calculator to audit seat overlaps and team minimum overflows (like Claude's 5-seat rule) for early startups. Completely free, no OAuth, takes 45 seconds. Thought it might save your current clients a few hundred bucks: [Link]. Let me know if the pricing math matches what you see."*
- Expected Conversion: 15 CTOs run audits across ~3 client stacks each = **45 users**.

### Track 2: The "Overspend Calling" X/Reddit Strategy (Days 11–20)
- Monitor X and Reddit search strings hourly for terms like "spent too much on API" or "Claude API bill".
- Respond with a helpful comment explaining the Claude Team 5-seat premium or API pooling rules, and attach the audit tool for confirmation.
- Expected Conversion: **25 users**.

### Track 3: VC Portfolio Manager Pitch (Days 21–30)
- Pitch the tool to 10 Early-stage VC "Portfolio Operations Managers". Their sole job is helping portfolio startups optimize run-rates.
- Suggest they share the free link in their monthly portfolio newsletter.
- Expected Conversion: **40 users**.

Because this tool is owned by **Credex**, we have an unfair acquisition loop:
**The Startup Credit Registry Data Sync.**
Credex already possesses contacts of companies looking to offload unused software seats. We can scrape LinkedIn for companies that recently announced pivots or downsized engineering teams, and email their operations desk: 
*"We have buyers ready to take your Cursor/ChatGPT Enterprise seats off your hands to save you from paying early contract termination fees. Run our 60-second Spend Audit to check what credits you can immediately offload."*
